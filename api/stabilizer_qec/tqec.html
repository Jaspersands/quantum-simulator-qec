stabilizer_qec.tqec

Block graphs of logical computations, as tqec draws them (tqec/tqec <https://github.com/tqec/tqec>_ 0.2.0's computation package, its gallery, its Collada and JSON files, its noise models, its simulation package and its PyZX interop, ported; Apache License 2.0, see LICENSE in this directory): cubes and pipes in a 3D spacetime diagram, ZX kinds, validation, port filling, composition, rotation, correlation surfaces (the logical observables) found on the graph's own ZX graph, and .dae files SketchUp and tqec exchange, read and written with the standard library.

Graphs compile as tqec compiles them, by a port of tqec's compile pipeline (its plaquettes, templates, layer trees and observables, with tqecd's detectors): compile_block_graph(graph, convention=...) and generate_stim_circuit(k=..., noise_model=NoiseModel.uniform_depolarizing(p)) give tqec's circuit at distance 2k + 1, byte for byte (a CNOT at k = 1: 81 qubits, 256 detectors, 2 observables), Hadamard pipes and spatial junctions included. compile_block_graph(graph, distance) compiles instead on this package's lattice-surgery engine (its own patch layout, its detectors, the same observables).

  • simulation: start_simulation_using_sinter, binary_search_threshold and the plots, on stabilizer_qec.sinter;
  • interop.pyzx: PyZX graphs read and written (ZXGraph.g, PositionedZX, block_synthesis, CorrelationSurface.to_pauli_web) when PyZX is installed.
>>> from stabilizer_qec import tqec
>>> g = tqec.gallery.cnot(tqec.Basis.Z)
>>> [s.external_stabilizer_on_graph(g) for s in g.find_correlation_surfaces()]
['ZZII', 'IZZZ']
>>> circuit = tqec.compile_block_graph(g).generate_stim_circuit(k=1, noise_model=tqec.NoiseModel.uniform_depolarizing(0.001))
>>> circuit.num_detectors, circuit.num_observables
(256, 2)

def compile_block_graph( graph: BlockGraph, distance=None, observables: Union[Sequence[CorrelationSurface], str, NoneType] = 'auto', block_temporal_height: object = None, *, convention: object = Convention(name='fixed_bulk'), p: float = 0.0, rounds: Optional[int] = None, merged: Optional[int] = None):

A block graph compiled to circuits, in either of two shapes.

tqec's: compile_block_graph(graph, convention=..., observables=..., block_temporal_height=...) (the second argument may be the convention, as tqec places it) is a TopologicalComputationGraph, compiled by tqec's pipeline (with tqec's errors), whose generate_stim_circuit(k, noise_model=...) is tqec's circuit at distance 2k + 1. block_temporal_height is a LinearFunction of k, the rounds between a block's initialisation and measurement layers (default LinearFunction(2, -1)).

This package's engine: compile_block_graph(graph, distance, p=..., rounds=..., merged=...) is the CompiledBlockGraph at that distance under SD6 noise p (the engine's noise model), with rounds rounds per time slice (default distance) and merged rounds per merge (default rounds): its circuit, tiles, steps and observables' terms.

observables are the correlation surfaces to include, as in tqec: "auto" (the default) all the graph has, as find_correlation_surfaces orders them, None none, or a list. The graph's shadowed faces are fixed first (fix_shadowed_faces), as tqec does, so a pass-through drawn as a spatial cube compiles as the patch it is. On the engine, a list's surfaces must be the graph's, and a surface that runs one patch's logical along two of its lines (it passes two seams of that patch) is refused: the surgery engine's observables take one line, and the difference of two lines is a band of checks whose signs are random.

def si1000(p: float) -> NoiseModel:

Superconducting-inspired noise, as "A Fault-Tolerant Honeycomb Memory" (arXiv:2108.10457) defines it, with the measurement result flipped instead of the qubit before it. Only Z-basis measurements and resets have a rule, as in tqec: a circuit that prepares or reads a patch in X is refused.

class TopologicalComputationGraph:

A block graph compiled as tqec's compile_block_graph compiles it, ready to be written at any scale: generate_stim_circuit(k, noise_model=...) is tqec's circuit at distance 2k + 1 (its plaquettes, its detectors, its observables), noiseless or with a noise model laid over it, and compile(distance, p=...) the program and circuit of this package's surgery engine under its own SD6 noise.

tqec's checks are made when the graph is compiled, with tqec's errors (open ports, validity, a Y half cube's NotImplementedError, a constant block_temporal_height); the engine's when compile is called. surfaces are the correlation surfaces compiled into observables and stabilizers the Pauli string each measures on the graph's ports (or leaf cubes). rounds and merged control the engine. Direct generation checks them against tqec's 2k + 1 layout and refuses with a ValueError any time slices it cannot honour, just as CompiledBlockGraph.generate_stim_circuit does. The check is arithmetic on the graph's time slices (one per cube height, a merge when a spatial pipe runs in it), so it holds on graphs the engine cannot express (spatial cubes, Hadamard pipes), whose matching controls pass through to tqec's circuit.

def compile( self, distance: int, *, p: float = 0.0) -> CompiledBlockGraph:

The surgery engine's program and circuit at distance under its SD6 noise p: each time slice block_temporal_height(k) + 2 rounds (tqec's layers: an initialisation layer, the repeated rounds and a measurement layer; 2k + 1 by default), or rounds and merged as given. Here block_temporal_height may also be a whole number at least 0 (a NumPy integer too) or a callable of k giving one. distance is an odd integer at least 3, a NumPy integer too; anything else (a float, a bool) is refused with a TQECError.

def generate_stim_circuit( self, k: int, noise_model=None, manhattan_radius: int = 2, detector_database=None, database_path=PosixPath('~/Library/Application Support/TQEC/detector_database.pkl'), do_not_use_database: bool = False, only_use_database: bool = False, reschedule_measurements: bool = True):

tqec's circuit at scale k (distance 2k + 1), with noise_model (ours, or tqec's own) laid over the noiseless circuit, as tqec lays it.

The detectors are found as tqec finds them, within manhattan_radius plaquettes (none when it is not positive), and reschedule_measurements is tqec's. tqec keeps the detectors it finds in a database file; here a database lives in memory only: detector_database (one from an earlier call's, else an empty one, or none with do_not_use_database), and database_path (by default tqec's database file, DEFAULT_DETECTOR_DATABASE_PATH) is taken for tqec's signature and never read or written. With only_use_database a situation missing from a database that has entries is an error, as in tqec; an empty database (or none) is filled, as tqec fills it. Engine rounds/merged overrides are checked against 2k + 1 and refused when tqec's layout cannot honour them; use compile(distance).circuit for those engine controls.

def fill_ports_for_minimal_simulation( graph: BlockGraph, search_small_area_observables: bool = False) -> list[FilledGraph]:

Fill the ports so that the fewest simulation runs cover the whole logical observable set: stabilizers that agree on the ports they share can be simulated together, so the minimum clique cover of the compatibility graph (a greedy colouring of its complement) gives the runs; each run's ports are filled in the bases its stabilizers need (Z where none cares).

def find_correlation_surfaces( g: ZXGraph, vertex_ordering: Optional[Sequence[set[int]]] = None, parallel: bool = False) -> list[CorrelationSurface]:

The correlation surfaces of a ZX graph, as tqec finds them: for each connected component, from its smallest leaf, explore node by node keeping a generating set of surfaces of the explored subgraph (at a node of basis B, the broadcast rule: all or none of its edges carry the opposite of B; the passthrough rule: an even number carry B; loops repaired by XOR-ing invalid candidates; dependent ones pruned), then reform the generators to satisfy the closed ports (an X or Z leaf supports only the opposite operator, a Y leaf only Y, a boundary any) and to keep few Y-terminating surfaces; the components' generators combined.

The result is sorted by span. g is this package's ZX graph or a PyZX graph (tqec's argument). vertex_ordering is reserved, as in tqec; parallel is tqec's multiprocessing of the independent parts, which gives the same surfaces (here they run in one process).
