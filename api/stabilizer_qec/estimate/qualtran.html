stabilizer_qec.estimate.qualtran

Qualtran's surface-code cost models (qualtran.surface_code 0.7.0): a QEC scheme (logical error rate from distance), physical parameters, data blocks (Gidney–Fowler's simple block; Litinski's compact, intermediate and fast blocks), magic-state factories (Gidney–Fowler's catalyzed CCZ-to-2T factory, Litinski's 15-to-1 with its density-matrix error model and its channels, several factories at once), rotation cost models, the PhysicalCostModel that combines them, and the Gidney–Fowler spreadsheet's and Beverland et al.'s estimates.

Every name is here, as in qualtran.surface_code, and in the submodule Qualtran keeps it in, under the same submodule names: algorithm_summary, beverland_et_al_model, ccz2t_factory, data_block, fifteen_to_one_factory, gidney_fowler_model, magic_state_factory, multi_factory, physical_cost_model, physical_cost_summary, physical_parameters, qec_scheme, rotation_cost_model, t_factory_utils and ui (the Dash app's estimates and figures without Dash; imported on its own, as Qualtran's is). GateCounts (Qualtran's qualtran.resource_counting) and TComplexity (qualtran.cirq_interop) are here too.

>>> from stabilizer_qec.estimate.qualtran.gidney_fowler_model import get_ccz2t_costs_from_grid_search
>>> from stabilizer_qec.estimate.qualtran import GateCounts
>>> cost, factory, data_block = get_ccz2t_costs_from_grid_search(n_logical_gates=GateCounts(t=10**8, toffoli=10**8), n_algo_qubits=100)
>>> factory, data_block, cost.footprint
(CCZ2TFactory(distillation_l1_d=15, distillation_l2_d=25), SimpleDataBlock(data_d=29, routing_overhead=0.5), 378700)

class FifteenToOne(stabilizer_qec.estimate.qualtran.MagicStateFactory):

Litinski's 15-to-1 T factory (arXiv:1905.06903): d_X and d_Z the patch sides, d_m the measurement rounds per surgery; its failure and output error from the paper's density-matrix error model of the eleven π/8 rotations.

The density matrix is evolved here with numpy, not with Cirq's simulator as in Qualtran, so the last bits of p_fail and p_out can differ from Qualtran's: p_fail agrees to about 1e-11 relative, and p_out (one minus a trace near 1) to about 1e-16 absolute, which is a large relative difference when p_out is itself near 1e-14 (a few percent at d_X=9, d_Z=5, d_m=3 and a 1e-5 physical error). Everything else here, n_cycles included, has given Qualtran's numbers exactly.
