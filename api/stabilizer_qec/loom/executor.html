stabilizer_qec.loom.executor

class EkaToStimConverter(Converter):

Loom's Stim converter: QUBIT_COORDS for the quantum channels sorted by label, each unrolled tick's gates grouped by operation (and noise) in order of first appearance, then the detectors (DETECTOR(x, y, t)) and OBSERVABLE_INCLUDE lines. With error_models (Loom's circuit error models, made on the circuit converted), each model's noise goes before or after the gates it gives a probability, on the group's targets (a later model's before-noise first), and the tick-wise models' noise after each tick's gates, on every qubit, or on the qubits idle for equal times together. The text is Loom's: what Stim refuses (a tick-wise model at probability zero writes its channel with no arguments) this package's parser refuses when the circuit is made.

def stim_polygons( interpreted_eka: stabilizer_qec.loom.InterpretationStep) -> str:

Crumble's #!pragma POLYGON lines for the stabilizers of the blocks at the start: red for X, green for Y, blue for Z, on the data qubits (the second half of the stabilizer's qubits, then the first half reversed). class EkaToGuppylangConverter(Converter):

Guppy (guppylang) code: q an array of qubits and c of booleans in label order, gates in tick order with Loom's start and end comments, a measurement a measure_and_reset into its bit and a result named after the bit's label, a SWAP three CXs, and the qubits discarded at the end. The prefixes (default guppyQ. for guppylang.std.quantum, guppyQSys. for guppylang.std.qsystem, builtins. for guppylang.std.builtins) must be empty or end with a dot; array is used bare.
