## Review Focus

1. **A user's own circuits and graphs, not the fixtures.** The tqec port runs on user graphs
   that tqec also compiles; `pm_exact` runs on user-built graphs with zero-weight and
   negative-weight edges and disconnected parts. These must behave as the reference does, or be
   refused with the reference's message. Owners: Tasks 2 and 3.
2. **The coherent sampler on circuits whose width exceeds the budget.** It must keep 2.1's
   truncation and say so (`exact_weights` False, the warning). It must never hang or allocate
   2^width. Owner: Task 1.
3. **Malformed inputs to new parsers.** ONNX protobufs and CUDA-Q chunk YAML, when truncated or
   carrying unknown ops or fields, must raise the reference's error (or a ValueError naming the
   field), never an IndexError or KeyError. Owners: Tasks 7 and 8.
4. **CLI misuse.** Missing required flags, unknown flags and unwritable `--out`/`--out_dir` must
   give the reference's messages and non-zero exits. Owners: Tasks 4, 5 and 12.
5. **Determinism across processes and Python versions.** Fixed seeds must give the same
   output: tqec's set order on 3.9–3.14, `pm_exact` across threads, and contraction weights
   independent of thread count. Owners: Tasks 1, 2 and 3.
