# 2.0 W9: Clifford+T at scale, and the headline — implementation plan

**Goal:** the spec's W9 (inventory T1–T8): a sampler for Clifford+T circuits at any qubit count
whose cost grows with the number of non-Clifford gates (Tsim's territory), the rest of Tsim's
circuit surface, and the 2.0 headline: Clifford+T circuits with coherent noise on every gate,
which neither Tsim (exponential in the non-Clifford count) nor the 1.9 coherent sampler (a
Clifford reference circuit) can sample.

**Where things stand.**
- T7 (Tsim's encoders) is done (W7: `stabilizer_qec.tsim`). `tsim_to_native` reads Tsim's
  tagged Stim (`S[T]`, half-turn angles) as this package's tags (`I[T]`, radians);
  `shorthand_to_stim` reads Tsim's shorthand (`T 0`, `TPP X0*Z1`, `CCZ 0 1 2`, `R_X(0.25) 0`).
- The non-Clifford tags (`nonpauli.rs`) are read by the state vector (≤ 24 qubits), which runs
  T, U3 and rotations exactly, and by the coherent sampler, which takes rotations only.
- The coherent sampler (1.9, `src/coherent/`) samples the Pauli twirl of a circuit's rotations
  and reweights each shot by the interference the twirl drops: the sum over the circuit's
  spacetime kernel (sets of rotations no record tells apart). Its identity is proved
  exhaustively against the state vector in `coherent/exact.rs`.
- T1–T6 and T8 are gaps or parts: no sampler beyond 24 qubits for circuits with T gates;
  `to_matrix`, `tcount`, `is_clifford`, `inverse` of tagged gates and the non-deterministic
  observable DEM are missing; `compile_sampler` on a circuit with `I[T]` silently runs Stim's
  reading (the identity).

**References.**
- bloqade-tsim 0.1.5 (Apache 2.0; its wheel and sdist in the scratchpad, installed in a venv
  of its own with JAX for the statistical check). Tsim builds a ZX diagram, doubles it, and
  decomposes it by stabilizer rank (pyzx-param); its samples are statistical, so distributions
  are compared within error bars. Its gate conventions (`test/helpers/gate_matrices.py`), its
  `is_clifford` rules and `inverse` are matched exactly.
- The state vector is the exact reference below 24 qubits, bit for bit.

**Architecture.** The sampler is new Rust, `src/stabrank.rs`, with the sum over Clifford
branches kept in the Pauli-coset form the frame sampler already lives in:

- A noiseless reference run: an Aaronson–Gottesman tableau (stabilizer and destabilizer rows
  with signs, bit-packed) through the circuit's Clifford gates and measurements, random
  outcomes taken as 0 (Stim's reference sample). It is the same in every shot.
- The state is Σ_k c_k D(k)|ref⟩ over *keys* k ∈ F₂ⁿ: k is a Pauli's anticommutation pattern
  with the stabilizer generators, which names its coset modulo the stabilizer group, and D(k) is
  the coset's canonical representative, the product of the destabilizers k selects. Distinct
  keys are orthogonal states; a Clifford gate changes neither keys nor coefficients (it
  conjugates the rows); a Pauli P maps D(k)|ref⟩ to λ·D(k ⊕ key(P))|ref⟩ for a phase λ found
  by one stabilizer-membership computation, memoised per (step, key) across shots since the
  reference is shot-independent.
- A non-Clifford gate is a sum of Paulis: exp(−iθP) = cos θ − i sin θ P splits every term in
  two (T = e^{iπ/8} exp(−iπ/8 Z); U3 is R_Z R_Y R_Z; a Clifford angle is a tableau operation
  and splits nothing). Terms landing on one key add: that is the interference, and the rank is
  the number of distinct cosets, at most 2^(T count) and far less on code states (15 T gates on
  a [[15,1,3]] code state are 32 terms).
- A measurement of Z_q: deterministic on the reference (Z_q in ±S) keeps the terms whose
  coset has the outcome; random collapses the reference as the tableau does and re-expresses
  each term in the new destabilizers (memoised), the outcome's probability being the squared
  norm of what remains. Resets are unread measurements followed by a frame correction.
- Pauli noise, feedback, heralds, pads and reset corrections live in a per-shot Pauli frame
  outside the terms, with the frame sampler's rules: a measurement's outcome flips where the
  frame anticommutes with it, a rotation's angle flips sign where the frame anticommutes with
  its Pauli.
- `exact_distribution` by this engine enumerates every noise draw and outcome (the state
  vector's odometer), which is how it is checked bit for bit.

The headline joins this engine to the coherent sampler: non-Clifford *gates* (`I[T]`,
`I[R_Z(…)]`, `I[U3(…)]`, on `I`/`II`) are exact terms; coherent *errors* (`I_ERROR[R_Z(…)]`,
`II_ERROR[R_ZZ(…)]`) are drawn as the twirl and weighted. For a shot with drawn error set S and
record m, the weight is the coherent probability of the shot's class over the twirl's: the class
is S ⊕ G over the kernel's projection onto the error locations (found by the 1.9 kernel search
with every non-Clifford gate counted as a location too); each member's final state is the term
engine re-run with the member's Paulis inserted and the shot's outcomes forced; the weight is
‖Σ_G a_{S⊕G}|Ψ_{S⊕G}⟩‖² / Σ_G |a_{S⊕G}|²‖Ψ_{S⊕G}‖², the overlaps being dot products over
shared keys. Pure-gate kernel elements (T-gate interference) need no member: the terms already
hold them exactly. The truncation to local generators and `order` is 1.9's; the exact harness
checks the identity with the whole kernel.

## Tasks

1. **The stabilizer-rank engine** (T1, T2, T3 via Tsim's shorthand, T4).
   - `src/stabrank.rs`: the tableau, keys and terms above; steps for every `Instr` the state
     vector runs except damping and leakage (refused with a pointer to the engine that runs
     them); `Program::sample_seeded` (one stream per shot, as every sampler here) and
     `Program::distribution`; `probability_of` (forced outcomes; exact without noise channels,
     an average over drawn noise with them).
   - Python: `Circuit.compile_stabilizer_rank_sampler(seed=…)` → `StabilizerRankSampler`
     (`sample` as the detector sampler's, `sample_measurements`, `probability_of`, `rank`
     statistics); `exact_distribution(method="stabilizer_rank")`;
     `compile_sampler`/`compile_detector_sampler` on a circuit with non-Clifford gates run this
     engine (a circuit's samplers run its gates; `without_tags()` for Stim's reading).
   - Rust facade: `StabilizerRankSampler` in `src/api`.
   - Checks: `exact_distribution` equal to the state vector's (1e-12) on random circuits of
     Clifford gates, T, T_DAG, rotations, U3, R_PAULI, Pauli channels, measurements in both
     bases, MR, resets, MPAD, heralds and feedback (≤ 10 qubits); sampled rates within error
     bars of the exact ones; rank bounds (T on |0⟩ is one term; a T on each qubit of a code state
     collapses to the X-stabilizer span); the sampler at 100+ qubits with few T gates runs;
     seeded shots are thread-independent and fingerprinted.
2. **Tsim's circuit surface** (T5, T6).
   - `Circuit.tcount()` (T gates and rotations by an odd multiple of π/4 — of π/8 in this
     package's exp(−iθP) — as pyzx counts them), `Circuit.is_clifford` (Tsim's rule: every
     angle a multiple of π/2 in half-turns, i.e. of π/4 here), `Circuit.to_matrix(endian=…)`
     and `to_tensor()` by the state vector on each basis state (≤ 10 qubits; no measurement,
     reset or noise), `Circuit.inverse()` on tagged gates (T ↔ T_DAG, θ → −θ,
     U3(θ, φ, λ)⁻¹ = U3(−θ, −λ, −φ)), `Circuit.detector_error_model(
     allow_non_deterministic_observables=True)` as Tsim's (observables become detectors under
     `allow_gauge_detectors`, then are mapped back; gauge errors on observables alone dropped).
   - `stabilizer_qec.tsim.Circuit(text)`: a circuit from Tsim's shorthand or tagged text
     (`shorthand_to_stim` then `tsim_to_native`), so Tsim's programs run as written.
   - Checks: Tsim's gate matrices (`gate_matrices.py`, every gate, TPP, R_*, U3, CCZ, CCX) equal
     `to_matrix` of the converted text; Tsim's `test_clifford.py`, `test_dem.py` and the
     `test_circuit.py` cases of these methods ported; `inverse` undoes the circuit on the state
     vector.
3. **Against Tsim, and timings.**
   - `tools/clifford_t.py check`: Tsim's demo circuits (the 5-qubit magic-state distillation,
     noisy and noiseless; the [[17,1,5]]-encoded distillation through `ColorEncoder5`; its
     integration tests' circuits) sampled by both; distributions compared by a chi-square test;
     wall time per shot for each; results in `data/clifford_t/tsim_check.json`.
   - `tests/test_tsim_differential.py`: the same on small cases, skipped when Tsim is not
     installed (its JAX stack stays out of CI's venv).
4. **The joint sampler** (the headline's engine).
   - `NonPauli::Rotation` learns whether it was written as a gate or an error (`I` vs
     `I_ERROR`); `coherent::Program` counts every non-Clifford op as a location (T and U3 as
     their rotations) so the kernel search sees them, and marks which are exact.
   - `src/coherent/joint.rs`: a shot draws the error locations as the twirl, runs the term
     engine with them in its frame, samples the record from the exact terms, then re-runs the
     engine for each class member (distinct error-location sets from the kernel elements the
     shot touches, as `Kernel::weight` chooses them) with the outcomes forced, and weights the
     shot as above. `compile_coherent_sampler` dispatches to it when the circuit has
     non-Clifford gates; the API is unchanged.
   - `coherent/exact.rs` extended: every branch of a small circuit with T gates and coherent
     errors (noise draws, firings, outcomes), each weighted by the whole projected kernel,
     summed per outcome, equals the state vector's distribution. Random circuits.
   - Checks: the exact harness; weighted rates within error bars of the state vector's at ≤ 24
     qubits (tests ≤ 10); the twirled circuit's rates differ where they should.
5. **The headline result.**
   - `tools/clifford_t.py inject|cultivate`: magic-state injection into a d = 3 colour-code
     patch and a d = 3 cultivation-style T check (the logical (X + Y)/√2 measured with an
     ancilla and transversal T/T†, postselected), every gate followed by an over-rotation of
     angle ε (one-qubit `I_ERROR[R_Z]`/`[R_X]`, two-qubit `II_ERROR[R_ZZ]`), at several ε:
     the magic state's infidelity by (a) the twirl (Pauli channels of probability sin²ε, the
     stabilizer-rank sampler), (b) the joint coherent sampler with its effective sample size,
     (c) the state vector where it fits (d = 3 fits; it is the proof); then d = 5 by (a) and
     (b) alone. Results in `data/clifford_t/*.json`.
   - README: a section "Clifford+T circuits, and coherent noise on every gate" with the
     numbers (tables through `tools/readme_tables.py`), the method, and what the twirl
     misjudges; CHANGELOG's headline paragraph.
6. **Bookkeeping.** CHANGELOG (T1–T8: T8, GPU, stays a gap and says so), the inventory,
   THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES (Tsim's DEM port), `_core.pyi`, the API docs, fingerprints for the new
   samplers.

Each task: tests first where the reference makes that possible. One commit each, and push.
