# 2.0 W5: decoder options and extras — implementation plan

**Goal:** every option, attribute and companion tool of ldpc 2.4.1, relay-bp 0.2.2, tesseract and
chromobius that 1.8 left out, bit-exact against them where they are deterministic.

**Architecture.** The algorithms are Rust (`src/bp.rs`, `src/osd.rs`, `src/lsd.rs`,
`src/relay.rs`, new `src/flip.rs`, `src/simplex.rs`), exposed through `src/py_api.rs` and
the ldpc-style classes in `python/stabilizer_qec/_decoders.py`. ldpc's pure-Python parts
(`mod2`, `code_util`, `codes`, `alist`, `protograph`, `noise_models`, `monte_carlo_simulation`,
`ckt_noise`, `sinter_decoders`) become `stabilizer_qec.ldpc`, ported (MIT, notices kept) onto
this package's decoders; `stabilizer_qec.ldpc` also exports the decoders under ldpc's names.

**Check.** Bit-exact differential tests against the installed references on random matrices and
generated circuits (decodings, posteriors to rounding, iteration counts); fixed-point Relay-BP
to the integer.

## Tasks

1. BP options (L1–L3, C4): `schedule` parallel / serial / serial_relative, random serial order
   (`random_schedule_seed`, `serial_schedule_order`), `input_vector_type="received_vector"`,
   the attributes (`log_prob_ratios`, `channel_probs`, `update_channel_probs`, `decoding`,
   `bp_decoding`, `osd0_decoding`, `osdw_decoding`, `omp_thread_count`, ...), and
   `osd_init_method` where ldpc has it, on `BpDecoder`, `BpOsdDecoder`, `BpLsdDecoder`.
2. ldpc's other decoders (L4–L9): `BeliefFindDecoder`, `UnionFindDecoder` on any matrix
   (peeling, inversion), `LsdDecoder`, `BpFlipDecoder`, `mbp_decoder`.
3. `stabilizer_qec.ldpc` (L10–L15): `mod2`, `code_util`, `codes`, `alist`, `protograph`,
   noise models, Monte Carlo simulations, overlapping-window decoders (`ckt_noise`) and the
   sinter decoders.
4. Relay-BP (R1–R3, C4): every precision (F32, F64, fixed point I8–I64), `decode_detailed`,
   `ObservableDecoderRunner`, gamma ensembles, `relay_solutions`.
5. Tesseract (R4–R6): `decode_to_errors`, `cost_from_errors`, `low_confidence_flag`, the
   `SimplexDecoder` and the utilities (`dem_from_counts`, `merge_indistinguishable_errors`,
   `remove_zero_probability_errors`, `build_det_orders`, `build_detector_graph`).
6. Chromobius weighted outputs (R7), `BeliefMatching.from_stim_circuit` (R8); CHANGELOG.

## Status

Done: tasks 1–5. Notes:
- Where ldpc's results depend on the C++ library, libc++/Apple libc, libstdc++/glibc and
  Microsoft's STL are reproduced (shuffle, `std::sort`, `std::hash<int>` in robin-map); only
  Microsoft's `qsort` (OSD's ties on Windows) is approximated.
- `mbp_decoder`'s C++ is not in ldpc 2.4.1; it is checked against `mbp.hpp` from ldpc's main
  branch, built by `tools/mbp_reference.cpp`.
- Merges of three or more LSD clusters at once follow ldpc's address order, which cannot be
  reproduced; such decodes are flagged (`last_tied`).
- Relay-BP's every number type comes from making the engine generic (`RelayNum`); f64 results
  are unchanged.
- Tesseract (task 5) is checked against its 2026-10-13 build, which is newer than the plan:
  it adds the multi-pass decoder, `demutil` and sparsified activation, all ported. The simplex
  decoder's programs are solved by an exact search, not HiGHS: the same optimal cost, and ties
  may differ (none did in the tests). Porting it found that editing a `DetectorErrorModel` in
  Python rounded its numbers to 16 digits; fixed at the root (exact text for copies).
