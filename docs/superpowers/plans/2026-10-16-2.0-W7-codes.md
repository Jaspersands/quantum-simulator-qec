# 2.0 W7: codes and code tools — implementation plan

**Goal:** every code family and code tool the inventory lists (spec W7: Q1–Q12, Q15–Q18, M1–M4,
M8, M11, U1, U2, U7, U9, D7, D8, C11, T7), each checked against its reference.

**Where things stand.** W6 ported qLDPC 0.4.1 whole (`stabilizer_qec.qldpc`) and MQT QECC
2.0.0 whole (`stabilizer_qec.mqt_qecc`), both passing their own tests. Between them they hold:
- Q1–Q18: classical and quantum families (Q1, Q2), qudit codes (Q3), non-CSS and subsystem codes
  and SWEL (Q4), logical bases (Q5), Brouwer–Zimmermann and random-search distances (Q6),
  equivalence (Q7), groups and rings (Q8), encoders and logical tableaus (Q9), transversal gates
  (Q10), memory experiments with edge-colouring and AlphaSyndrome schedules (Q11, Q12),
  `DetectorErrorModelArrays` (Q15), code-capacity models and `ErrorRateFunc` (Q16),
  experimental surgery with bridges and Cheeger constants (Q17), and opt-in qecdb, GAP and
  sqetch (Q18);
- M1–M4, M8, M11: encoder synthesis, fault-tolerant state preparation, cat states, Clifford and
  CNOT synthesis, concatenated and further codes, fault sets.

What is left is four packages no one has ported and two small pieces:
- QUITS 1.2.0 (U1, U2: BB, BPC, HGP, LCS, Mitten and QLP codes, circuit builders, sliding-window
  BP+OSD and BP+LSD), MIT, 6.8k lines;
- PanQEC 0.1.7 (U7: 3D toric, rhombic, X-cube and deformed codes, their decoders, threshold
  analysis, the GUI), MIT, 19k lines;
- PECOS 0.2.0, `quantum-pecos` (U9: fault-tolerance checks, pseudo-thresholds, error
  generators, 4.4.4.4 surface and 4.8.8 colour codes, density- and process-matrix simulators),
  Apache-2.0, 19k lines;
- Deltakit explorer 0.9.4 with deltakit-circuit and deltakit-core 0.9.2 (D7, D8: planar, toric,
  repetition, BB, generic CSS and stabiliser codes, schedules, memory and stability
  experiments), Apache-2.0, 32k lines (its analysis is W4's `stabilizer_qec.analysis`);
- CUDA-Q QEC's surface code with its four orientations XV, XH, ZV, ZH (C11), from cudaqx's
  `surface_code.cpp`;
- Tsim's encoders (T7: `TransversalEncoder`, `SteaneEncoder`, `ColorEncoder5`), 260 lines.

**Architecture.** As W6: each package ported whole as a drop-in subpackage
(`stabilizer_qec.quits`, `.panqec`, `.pecos`, `.deltakit`), its imports of Stim, ldpc,
PyMatching and sinter pointed at this package, its own tests passing on it, its third-party
needs an extra. Where a reference runs here, differential tests compare outputs on randomised
inputs (bit for bit where deterministic).

## Tasks

1. **Check-off and cross-checks** (Q1–Q18, M1–M4, M8, M11): the inventory updated; and the
   spec's own checks across the two ports and the engine: on every qLDPC family small enough,
   [[n, k, d]] by qLDPC's Brouwer–Zimmermann, by this package's MILP distance and by MQT where it
   has the code, all equal; each qLDPC and MQT encoder verified on this package's `Tableau`
   (its stabilizers and logicals map as claimed).
2. **QUITS** (U1, U2): `stabilizer_qec.quits`, its tests (from its repository) passing, and its
   codes' parity-check matrices, circuits and window decodes equal to quits' own.
3. **PanQEC** (U7): `stabilizer_qec.panqec`, its tests passing; codes' stabilizers and logicals
   equal to panqec's, its decoders' corrections equal on the same seeds; the GUI under an extra
   (Flask).
4. **PECOS** (U9): `stabilizer_qec.pecos`, quantum-pecos 0.2.0's tests passing; fault checks
   and simulators equal to PECOS's own.
5. **Deltakit** (D7, D8): `stabilizer_qec.deltakit` (`circuit`, `core`, `explorer` codes, `qpu`
   experiments, types and plotting), offline (the cloud client raises clearly); circuits equal
   to deltakit's text for every code and experiment, memory and stability.
6. **Orientations and encoders** (C11, T7): `cudaq_qec`'s `surface_code` with XV, XH, ZV, ZH
   (and Ising's O1–O4), its stabilizers, observables and CNOT orders as cudaqx's;
   `memory_circuit(..., orientation=)`; Tsim's encoders on this package's `Circuit`, verified by
   `Tableau`. CHANGELOG.
