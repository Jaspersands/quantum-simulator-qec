QEC Threshold Curves

Pure Data Qubit Noise: Evaluates the Union-Find decoder under perfect stabilizer measurements (1 round). A distinct threshold is observed at p ≈ 8.0%. Below this threshold, larger code distances exponentially suppress the logical error rate.

Phenomenological Spacetime Noise: Simulates d rounds of stabilizer measurements where both physical data qubits and stabilizer measurements fail. The threshold is located at p ≈ 1.5%.

Gottesman-Knill Simulator

Simulates Clifford circuits in polynomial time. Rows of stabilizers and destabilizers are packed into CPU words, executing rowsum arithmetic via fast bitwise XOR and shift operations.

🔍

Peeling Decoder

The Union-Find algorithm clusters syndrome defects and grows them until they are even. A spanning tree is constructed on the cluster, and leaf nodes are recursively peeled to find the correction matching the exact error path.

⚡

Blindingly Fast

Written in Rust, achieving 13,000+ spacetime stabilizer checks per second and 75,000+ pure data QEC checks per second, allowing rapid threshold searches and high-precision sampling.
